Our heritage

Jineshwar Stone Impex

Based in Fatehpur Beri, New Delhi, the business brings more than 30 years of stone expertise to international exports, sourcing and bespoke fabrication. Founder Rajesh Jain has more than 40 years of personal experience in the industry.

Its specializations include Indian marble blocks and slabs, carved objects, inlay, architectural tables and custom stonework.

Purpose & direction

Material knowledge meets craft.

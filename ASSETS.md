Product images illustrate sample catalogue records. Confirm the actual products, specifications, colours, stock and commercial photography with the business before using the site for customer orders. ## Fonts and icons

Poppins and Cormorant Garamond are Google Fonts distributed under the SIL Open Font License. Latin WOFF2 subsets are served locally and their license texts are included in `public/fonts/`. The local Lucide SVG icons and license appear in `public/icons/`.

## Design Studio room studies

Five additional original AI-generated editorial room illustrations live in `public/images/rooms/`. Each has a plain application surface and is resized to a maximum width of 1100 pixels. The room keys are living (living room floor), kitchen (island worktop), bathroom (plaster wall behind the tub), bedroom (bedroom floor) and wall (foyer accent wall). The visualizer composites the selected local catalogue texture into a hand-traced polygon while retaining scene luminance. The comparison uses the same camera view and surface mask for both states. These are design studies, not company installations or product colour guarantees.

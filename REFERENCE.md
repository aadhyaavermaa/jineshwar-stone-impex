# Reference website audit

Reviewed 24 November 2026. The original site is a client-rendered React single page. Its public HTML provided the page title and description and identified the JavaScript asset rendering the visible sections. The audit used that asset to check the displayed text, controls and outbound links.

[Company homepage](https://jineshwarstoneimpex.com/) · [Public page implementation at time of review](https://jineshwarstoneimpex.com/assets/index-CSb_sogZ.js)

## Content and interactions

| Original section | Findings |
| --- | --- |
| Navigation | Desktop and mobile navigation link to Home, About, Expertise, Collection and Contact within the same page. The hero links to the collection and heritage sections. |
| Identity | The wordmark combines the company name with serif lettering. Stone neutrals, architectural photography, contrasting light and dark sections and editorial headings establish the visual identity. |
| Background | The company describes a combination of traditional carving and contemporary design, precision fabrication, inlay and custom furniture. Its website reports more than 30 years of experience and an international export reach. Founder Rajesh Jain is credited with more than 40 years of personal industry experience. |
| Consultancy | The original covers material and application selection, identifying suppliers, comparing value and quality, selecting slabs and blocks, matching veins, fabrication and finishes, installation advice and guidance across the project. Marble, granite and travertine appear as material examples. |
| Services | The five headings cover export of crafted stone objects, export of Indian marble blocks and slabs, handicraft export for interiors and hospitality, stone sourcing advice and individually commissioned architectural pieces. |
| Collection | Architectural tables appear under furniture. Bathroom sets, marble sculptures and sculptural bookends appear under handicrafts. A catalogue-request link leads to the contact section. |
| Material exploration | A control switches between an architectural application and a draggable raw-slab view with notes on veins, crystals, origin and finishes. The separate stone archive has ten images. Most lack individual variety names or specifications in the accessible markup. |
| Social and contact | A four-tile Instagram preview, Instagram and LinkedIn links, floating WhatsApp and Instagram links, and the footer provide the main contact routes. |
| Original transaction model | The navigation, collection and contact CTAs lead to informational sections or external channels. The inspected UI contains no checkout, authenticated inventory management, stored enquiries or customer status tracking. |

## Content boundaries

The site provides no opening hours, authenticated customer reviews, individual stock levels, current prices, confirmed on-hand product dimensions or certifications in the inspected content. The contact address supports a map search; exact business-pin coordinates were not established. The original describes design aspirations without publishing a separately labelled formal mission and vision. Project descriptions and captions should distinguish editorial material guidance from official company statements. Collection records, images and specifications created for the university demonstration require owner approval before commercial use. Original product photographs, logo artwork and customer project images have not been licensed for redistribution.
